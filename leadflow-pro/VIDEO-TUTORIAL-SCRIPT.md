## 2:30 - 4:00 | Step 2: The Smart Auditor (The Hook)
**Visual:** Screen recording of the 'Leads' CRM view and then clicking a lead to see the Audit results.
**Script:**
"Now, here is the secret sauce. Cold outreach works best when it's RELEVANT.

LeadFlow Pro’s background auditor automatically crawls every lead's website. It detects if they are missing an SSL certificate, if their site is slow, if it’s mobile-friendly, or if the design is outdated.

See this lead here? [Point to screen]. The auditor flagged 'No SSL' and 'Not Mobile Responsive.' That is a massive pain point for a business owner, and now we have the perfect 'hook' to get their attention."

## 4:00 - 6:00 | Step 3: AI-Powered Outreach
**Visual:** Screen recording of 'Campaigns' builder and the 'AI: Write this for me' button.
**Script:**
"Next, let’s automate the contact. I’ll build a new campaign. We’ll target all 'New' leads.

It says: 'Hi, I noticed your site AustinDental.com isn't mobile-friendly...' It sounds human, it's helpful, and it scales perfectly. We can add follow-up steps with 3-day delays to ensure no lead falls through the cracks."
